# Knowledge Atlas — working rules

- Purpose: help learners understand concepts and their relationships, not merely collect definitions.
- Use English scientific terms and definitions, with Egyptian Arabic teaching explanations. Establish necessary prerequisites before use.
- Separate a learning sequence from a knowledge classification. Reuse canonical concept identities; distinguish a part-of relationship from a prerequisite or related concept.
- Expand nodes recursively where evidence reveals missing concepts; record sources and unresolved gaps instead of inventing facts. The current map is provisional.
- Use clear illustrations, practical demonstrations, and questions that test understanding. Preserve image aspect ratios and attribute third-party assets according to their licenses.
- Publish only material actually written; label planned topics as such. Do not treat publication as learner approval.
- Keep the site usable without paid subscriptions or accounts. Never commit personal data, secrets, private files or unpublished private research to this public repository.
- Keep lessons accessible from the homepage, with publication dates and browser-local progress. Update the index when adding lessons. Verify links, rendering and interactions before claiming they work.
- Begin with static HTML/CSS/JS as a small reversible prototype; no permanent framework choice is implied.

## Continuity gates (27 November 2026)
- Before researching a topic, inspect the recovered source inventory and existing canonical or staged records. Reuse verified content instead of silently reconstructing absent upstream material.
- Preserve the distinction between recovered, staged, published, explicitly approved and rejected. Editing an approved version returns the new version to pending approval. Keep rejection evidence and the reason, correction and preventive rule.
- A file, commit, count or structural test does not prove educational quality or project completion. Check the actual rendered lesson, links, interactions and prerequisites; only the owner grants approval. Never count proposed nodes as completed lessons.
- Prior research includes Unified Atlas integration v0.1, semantic review v0.2 and evidence staging v0.3. No canonical merge was executed. The raw research archive is private; publish only reviewed, appropriately attributed learner-facing material. See recovery.html for current gaps.
- For a new lesson, link to existing knowledge IDs when meaning is verified; note missing prerequisites and conflicting senses without changing original IDs. Resolve important gaps at point of use, not by restarting the entire Atlas.
