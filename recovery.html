Rules that prevent repeated work

Preserve
Keep original IDs, source context, version, decisions and rejected evidence.
Distinguish
Tree for hierarchy; graph for overlaps. Browsing relationships are not automatically logical subclass relationships.
Teach
Build a useful lesson using recovered concepts and verified sources. Explain in Egyptian Arabic with original English technical terms.
Verify
Distinguish file existence, structural checks, visual/functional review and owner approval. Do not count drafts as completed learning.
