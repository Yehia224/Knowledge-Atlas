# Research — continuity and result tracking

Status: working convention / pending owner approval.

Research results are distinct from source files, canonical knowledge records and lessons. See [Source Library](../sources/README.md) and [public continuity summary](continuity-summary.md).

For every research round, retain question, scope, inherited IDs, sources and exact locators, findings, proposed nodes/edges, unresolved gaps, decisions, versions, validation results and next resume point. Use [round template](round.template.md). Use the existing Universal Structured Search Protocol; discover scope before deep research, independently search significant concepts, resolve existing identity before creating candidates, and use practical saturation instead of count targets.

Do not commit raw private research packages here. Public-safe continuity notes do not confer scientific approval, canonical authority, or release readiness. Full evidence packages belong in a private repository/workspace. A research result cannot become a lesson or an approved knowledge assertion simply because it is present in Git.
